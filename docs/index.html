Photo Organiser

Your photos, sorted
by date and place.

Choose your photos and get them back in tidy folders. Nothing to set up.

  • Your photos never leave this device. They are read and packed right here in your browser.
  • To name places, rounded GPS coordinates (about 1 km, never the photos) are sent to OpenStreetMap.
  • Photos without a date are sorted using the date in their filename, or when the file was saved.

On iPhone? In the photo picker tap Options and switch on Location, or iOS removes the place information from your photos.
