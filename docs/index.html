📷 Photo Organiser

Sorts copies of your photos into Date → Location folders using the date and GPS stored inside each photo. Your originals are never changed.

🔒 Everything runs in your browser, on this device. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

1 · Choose your photos

📱 iPhone: in the photo picker tap Options and turn on Location, otherwise iOS removes the GPS and photos can only be sorted by date.

Options

Each unique photo location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to the public OpenStreetMap Nominatim service together with your IP address – never the photos. Limited to one request per second, and remembered in this browser so repeats are not asked again. Leave this off to sort into date folders only.

Photos saved from chat apps often lose their date. These options only give a labelled ESTIMATE when no real date exists.

2 · Analyse

Reads each photo's capture date and GPS. Nothing is copied yet, and only the start of each file is read.

Your options changed since the last analysis. Click Analyse photos again before organising.
